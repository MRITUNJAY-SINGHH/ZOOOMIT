Delete Your ZOOOMIT Account

We value your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. Read below to understand how account deletion works and how to submit a deletion request.

Send Deletion Request

Important Notice on How Deletion Works:

Clicking the button above opens your device's email application with a pre-filled deletion request template. Your account is NOT deleted automatically upon clicking the button. You must send the email to submit your request to our support desk. Our team will review the request and verify your account ownership prior to processing.

Security Safeguard — Never Share Credentials:

DO NOT send your account password, SMS verification OTPs, or payment card details in your email request. ZOOOMIT support personnel will never request your passwords or OTPs to process an account deletion.

1. What Account Deletion Means

When you request the deletion of your ZOOOMIT account:

  • Personal Profile Removal: Your customer profile details, including your full name, registered mobile phone number, and email address, will be unlinked and scheduled for permanent purge.
  • Saved Delivery Addresses: All saved addresses, apartment details, delivery instructions, and GPS coordinates stored in your address book will be deleted.
  • Loss of Account Access: You will no longer be able to log in to the ZOOOMIT app or website with your previous mobile number.
  • Active Carts & Wishlists: Saved grocery baskets, favorite items, and coupon redemptions will be permanently reset.

2. Pre-Requisites Before Requesting Deletion

To ensure seamless account closure without financial or delivery disputes, please confirm the following before submitting your request:

  • No Active Orders: All ongoing grocery deliveries must be completed or cancelled. Deletion cannot proceed while an order is actively out for delivery.
  • Settled Payments & Refunds: Any pending refunds, dispute claims, or Cash on Delivery reconciliations must be fully concluded.

3. Data Retained for Legal & Accounting Compliance

While your personal profile and marketing identifiers are permanently deleted, applicable laws require ZOOOMIT to retain certain operational and financial records:

  • Tax & Invoicing Records: Completed order invoices, GST accounting ledgers, and transaction reference numbers must be archived for mandatory statutory periods under commercial and taxation regulations.
  • Fraud Prevention & Audit: De-identified records of transactions may be preserved to prevent fraudulent abuse, chargeback disputes, and comply with legal subpoenas.

For detailed information regarding our data retention practices, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

4. Verification & Processing Timelines

  • Identity Verification: Upon receipt of your email, our privacy team may verify your identity by sending an SMS verification to your registered mobile phone number.
  • Processing Turnaround: Once ownership is authenticated, account deactivation is initiated immediately, with complete data purge concluded within our standard processing timeline of 7 to 14 business days.
  • Final Confirmation: A written confirmation email will be sent to notify you once your account has been successfully deleted.
