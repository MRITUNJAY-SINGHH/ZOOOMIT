  • FAQ

  • ZOOOMIT is an everyday grocery shopping and daily essentials delivery platform. You can browse fresh fruits, vegetables, dairy, pantry staples, snacks, and household products on our app or website, add items to your cart, and have them delivered directly to your doorstep in minutes.

  • We offer a comprehensive selection of everyday groceries, including Farm-Fresh Fruits & Vegetables, Dairy, Bread & Eggs, Pantry & Kitchen Staples (atta, basmati rice, dals, pulses, cooking oils, ghee, authentic spices), Packaged Snacks & Munchies, Cold Drinks & Juices, and Household Cleaning & Personal Care Essentials.

  • Delivery times depend on your locality and slot availability. Once your order is confirmed, our fulfillment partners carefully pack your items and dispatch them with our delivery partner. You will receive prompt status updates on your phone as your order is packed and dispatched.

  • ZOOOMIT is built for daily convenience with no rigid minimum order requirements. However, free delivery thresholds or nominal delivery charges may apply depending on your location, basket size, and active promotions displayed transparently at checkout.

  • Placing an order is effortless: 1. Open the ZOOOMIT app or website and log in using your mobile number. 2. Select or enter your delivery address. 3. Browse your favourite grocery aisles or use the search bar to add items to your cart. 4. Review your cart quantities and proceed to checkout. 5. Choose your payment method and confirm the order. You will receive instant confirmation and order updates.

  • Yes. We partner directly with verified suppliers and local distributors to source fresh produce daily. Perishable items such as dairy, bread, and greens are inspected for quality before dispatch and transported in hygienic, insulated packaging to maintain optimal freshness.

  • We accept all major secure payment options, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking across major banks, and Cash on Delivery (COD) where enabled for your area.

  • You receive prompt in-app and SMS status updates at every stage—from order confirmation and store packing to when your delivery partner is on the way to your doorstep.

  • If a product is temporarily unavailable, it will clearly show as 'Out of Stock' in the catalog. Our inventory updates throughout the day as fresh shipments arrive. You can add the item to your wishlist to receive a notification as soon as it is back on the shelf.

  • Customer satisfaction is our highest priority. If any item is damaged, leaking, incorrect, or missing, please report it within 24 hours of delivery through the 'Help & Support' section in the app or email us at aetern.india@gmail.com with your Order ID and photos. We will arrange a replacement or prompt refund.

  • We offer a hassle-free return experience. Perishable items like fruits, vegetables, and milk can be checked upon delivery and returned if quality expectations are not met. Non-perishable items can be returned within the specified window in original, sealed packaging. Please check our detailed Return & Refund Policy page for full terms.

  • Once approved, UPI and wallet refunds are typically credited within a few hours to 24 hours. Debit/Credit card and Net Banking refunds usually appear in your bank account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your banking institution's standard settlement process.

  • Yes. During checkout, you can select from available delivery time slots that fit your daily schedule, whether you want an early morning delivery before work or an evening delivery after getting home.

  • ZOOOMIT operates 7 days a week, from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends and public holidays. Specific operating hours in your neighborhood may vary slightly depending on partner store schedules.

  • We use specialized insulated thermal bags and ice-gel packs for temperature-sensitive items such as milk, butter, cheese, and frozen foods to ensure they stay cold and fresh during transit.

  • Never. ZOOOMIT strictly follows transparent billing. Your final order summary displays the exact cost of products, applicable taxes, and any delivery fee before you pay. There are no surprise handling fees or platform convenience surcharges added later.

  • You can cancel your order through the app as long as the store has not begun packing it. Once packing or dispatch begins, cancellations are restricted to prevent food waste. If you need urgent assistance, contact customer support immediately.

  • Simply enter your address or pincode on our homepage or app. If we currently serve your area, you can immediately browse the live product catalog. If we haven't reached your street yet, you can sign up for updates as we rapidly expand to new delivery zones.

  • On the cart or checkout screen, look for the 'Apply Coupon' section. Choose from our list of active promotional offers or enter a valid coupon code in the text box and click 'Apply'. The discount will be instantly deducted from your order total.

  • Yes. An account linked to your verified mobile number ensures that your delivery addresses are saved securely, your past orders are accessible for quick reordering, and you receive instant order status updates.

  • Yes. All payments are encrypted and processed through RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store your full card details or CVV. Your personal information is protected under our strict Privacy Policy.

  • Yes. You can save multiple addresses under labels like Home, Office, Parents, or Other in your profile. When placing an order, simply choose the destination address with one tap.

  • If you wish to delete your account, you can submit a request directly through our Delete Account page or email us at aetern.india@gmail.com. Your request will be verified and processed in compliance with statutory data privacy standards.

  • Our support team is always ready to assist you. You can reach out via the 'Help' section in the ZOOOMIT mobile app, visit our Contact Us page, or email us at aetern.india@gmail.com. We respond promptly to all customer queries.
