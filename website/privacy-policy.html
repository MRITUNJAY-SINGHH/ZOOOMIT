1. Introduction & Scope

ZOOOMIT (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our customers (“you”, “your”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect your information when you browse our website (zooomit), access our mobile applications, or place orders for daily groceries and household essentials.

By accessing or using our platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please refrain from using our services.

2. Personal Information We Collect

To fulfill everyday grocery orders reliably to your doorstep, we collect only the information necessary for processing transactions, doorstep navigation, and account security:

  • Account & Identity Details: Full name, registered mobile phone number, login email address, and SMS-based one-time password (OTP) verification records.
  • Delivery Addresses & Doorstep Telemetry: House/flat number, apartment/building name, street address, locality, landmark, city, state, postal code (PIN code), and delivery instructions (e.g., “leave at door”). With your explicit permission, we collect device GPS coordinates to verify delivery serviceability and guide delivery partners to your exact drop point.
  • Order & Transaction History: Grocery items added to cart, purchase preferences, order totals, invoice records, delivery time slots, and records of cancellation or support issues.
  • Customer Support Records: Communications, inquiries, issue tickets, and photographic evidence submitted for missing, damaged, or expired grocery items.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Device model, operating system version, IP address, app build version, and system error diagnostic logs collected to ensure app stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected strictly for legitimate commercial and fulfillment purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment: To pack, dispatch, and deliver fresh groceries and daily essentials to your specified delivery address.
  • Dispatch & Doorstep Verification: To match your delivery with an assigned delivery partner, generate live transit status updates, and verify order handover through our secure Delivery OTP system.
  • Serviceability Checks: To confirm whether your location falls within our serviceable delivery zones and store hubs.
  • Customer Care & Refunds: To resolve customer support tickets, process approved product returns or order cancellations, and disburse refunds.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: To detect fraudulent transactions, protect customer accounts, and comply with applicable statutory laws.

4. Payment Information & Security

All digital payments on ZOOOMIT are processed securely through RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay). We accept major credit/debit cards, Net Banking, Unified Payments Interface (UPI), and Cash on Delivery (COD) where available.

Important: ZOOOMIT does not store your complete payment card numbers, CVV codes, or UPI MPINs on our servers. All sensitive financial authorizations occur directly within the secure, encrypted frames of the payment provider.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing companies. Data is shared exclusively under strict confidentiality terms with:

  • Delivery Personnel: Your delivery address, customer name, and contact details are shared with the assigned delivery partner solely for the duration of transit and order handover.
  • Operational Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud database hosts, SMS transactional gateways (for OTP delivery), and mapping service providers required for distance calculation.
  • Legal Compliance: If mandated by law enforcement, statutory authorities, or court orders to comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your customer account remains active or as necessary to provide fulfillment services. Completed order histories, invoice ledgers, and taxation records are archived for the mandatory statutory period prescribed by applicable accounting and commercial regulations.

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. For full instructions on our deletion process, please visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page.

7. Data Security Standards

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal data. All data transmissions between your browser or mobile device and our backend servers are encrypted using 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL). Authentication sessions utilize secure, signed cryptographic tokens.

8. Privacy Policy Updates

ZOOOMIT reserves the right to update this policy periodically to reflect operational, technological, or legal changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated “Effective Date”. Continued use of our platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
