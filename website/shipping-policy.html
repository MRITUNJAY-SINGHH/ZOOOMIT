1. Overview & Service Commitment

At ZOOOMIT, our mission is to deliver fresh fruits, vegetables, dairy, household essentials, and daily groceries conveniently to your doorstep. This Shipping & Delivery Policy outlines our fulfillment procedures, address guidelines, delivery charges, and handover processes.

2. Serviceable Locations & Availability

ZOOOMIT operates through localized fulfillment partner hubs. We accept and fulfill orders exclusively within our designated serviceable PIN codes and geographical delivery boundaries.

  • Serviceability Check: Before placing an order, customers can verify delivery availability by selecting their delivery address or allowing location access in the application.
  • Non-Serviceable Areas: If your selected address falls outside our current active delivery perimeter, the platform will notify you that service is not yet available in your locality.
  • Note: Specific serviceable cities and expansion regions require platform owner confirmation.

3. Providing an Accurate Delivery Address

To ensure fast, reliable delivery and avoid missed drop-offs, customers must provide complete and precise address details:

  • House, flat, or villa number and building/apartment name.
  • Street address, block, or sector number.
  • Prominent landmark (e.g., “Near Metro Gate 2”, “Opposite Central Park”).
  • Correct 6-digit Postal PIN Code.
  • Accurate GPS location pin dropped on the in-app map.
  • Special delivery instructions (e.g., “Call on arrival”, “Leave with security guard”).

4. Estimated Delivery Timelines & Operating Hours

  • Operating Hours: Orders are processed and dispatched daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM across all operational grocery fulfillment hubs..
  • Dynamic ETA: An estimated delivery time (ETA) is computed dynamically when you place your order, factoring in store packing time, distance, courier availability, and traffic conditions.
  • No Unrealistic Guarantees: While we endeavor to fulfill groceries swiftly, ZOOOMIT does not offer unconditional minute guarantees during severe weather, traffic blockages, or technical outages.

5. Delivery Fees & Packaging Charges

  • Transparent Calculation: Any applicable delivery fee, platform fee, or packaging charge is calculated dynamically and itemized clearly on the order review screen before payment.
  • Free Delivery Threshold: Promotional free delivery minimum order criteria may apply depending on current campaigns Standard delivery fees apply, with promotional free delivery automatically activated on qualifying order totals at checkout..
  • Surge / Rain Fees: During extreme weather events or late-night operations, nominal surge charges may apply to fairly compensate delivery partners.

6. Order Packing & Courier Handover

Once your order is confirmed, store personnel carefully pick and pack items with strict quality checks. Perishable goods like milk, greens, and fruits are separated from household cleaners and packaged groceries. The packed order is then handed over to the assigned delivery partner for transit.

7. Doorstep Handover & Delivery OTP Verification

To protect against missed or misplaced deliveries:

  • Customer Presence: You or an authorized person must be present at the delivery address when the partner arrives.
  • Delivery OTP: The delivery partner will request the confidential 4-digit Delivery OTP displayed on your order screen. The order is marked “Delivered” in the system only after the correct OTP is verified.

8. Failed Delivery Attempts & Customer Unavailability

  • The delivery partner will attempt to contact you via telephone upon arrival at your specified location.
  • If you are unreachable for more than 10 minutes, or if an incorrect delivery address was provided, the order will be marked as a failed delivery.
  • Because grocery orders contain highly perishable products (dairy, fresh produce) that cannot be returned to shelf once dispatched, orders cancelled due to customer unavailability may not be eligible for a refund.

9. Weather & Operational Delays

Delivery timelines may occasionally be extended due to circumstances beyond our direct control, such as heavy rain, flooding, strikes, public events, technical dispatch outages, or sudden traffic diversions. We appreciate your patience during such events as we prioritize delivery partner road safety.
