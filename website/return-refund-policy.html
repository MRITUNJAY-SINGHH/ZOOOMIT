Customer Protection Policy
Effective Date: October 2026
v1.0
aetern.india@gmail.com

1. Overview

At ZOOOMIT, we take immense pride in delivering top-quality groceries, fresh farm produce, dairy, and household essentials. Because our catalog includes perishable food items with limited shelf lives, our return and refund guidelines balance strict hygiene standards with fair customer protection.

2. Eligible Reasons for Return or Refund

You are entitled to a replacement or refund under the following verified circumstances:

  • Damaged or Leaking Items: Packages crushed, torn, unsealed, or leaking during transit.
  • Spoiled or Defective Produce: Fruits or vegetables received rotten, wilted, or unfit for consumption.
  • Expired Products: Packaged groceries delivered past their stated “Best Before” or “Expiry” date.
  • Missing Items: Products billed on your invoice but omitted from the delivered parcel.
  • Incorrect Variant / Item: A different brand, size, or flavor delivered than what was ordered.

3. Ineligible Return Situations

Returns, replacements, or refunds will not be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Change of Mind: Cancellation or return requested after successful doorstep delivery of perishable food items.
  • Improper Storage: Items spoiled due to improper refrigeration or handling after doorstep handover (e.g., dairy, frozen foods left in heat).
  • Partially Consumed Goods: Products that have been used, opened, or consumed (unless reported immediately as spoiled upon unsealing).
  • Unreported Beyond Window: Issues reported after the applicable claim window has elapsed without valid substantiation.

4. Reporting Windows for Order Issues

Because grocery products deteriorate rapidly, claims must be registered promptly:

  • Perishable Groceries (Dairy, Fresh Vegetables, Fruits, Bread): Must be inspected at delivery or reported promptly within our standard grocery inspection window of 2 to 4 hours following delivery..
  • Non-Perishable Packaged Goods (Grains, Pulses, Snacks, Household Cleaners): Must be reported within 24 to 48 hours of delivery handover.

5. How to Submit an Issue & Evidence Required

You can raise a support issue directly through either of the following channels:

  • In-App Support Ticket: Navigate to your order details in the ZOOOMIT mobile app and tap “Raise an Issue” or “Report a Problem”.
  • Email Support: Send an email to aetern.india@gmail.com.

Please ensure your message contains:

  • Your unique Order ID (e.g., ZOM-10294).
  • Registered phone number and customer name.
  • Names and quantities of the affected items.
  • Clear photographs showing the defect, damage, or printed expiry date on the packaging.

6. Refund Review & Verification Process

Our support team verifies reported issues against store picking checklists, packing CCTV logs, and delivery partner OTP handover timestamps. If the issue is validated, our team will immediately approve a refund or schedule an exchange where stock permits.

7. Refund Disbursement Methods & Timelines

  • Prepaid Online Orders (UPI / Cards / Net Banking): Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original source instrument via our payment gateway (Razorpay). Depending on your bank or card issuer, funds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 operational banking days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds for COD transactions are settled via direct electronic bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), UPI ID transfer, or credited as store shopping balance, per customer choice.
  • Note: Exact bank settlement turnaround times depend on the customer's financial institution.

8. Pre-Dispatch Order Cancellations

If you cancel your order while it is still in “Placed” status prior to store packing and courier dispatch, 100% of the prepaid order value is automatically reversed to your original payment method.
